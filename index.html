Earlier teaching · Before NTU

Chung Yuan Christian University

Before joining NTU, Ryan taught as Adjunct Assistant Professor at the Center for General Education, Chung Yuan Christian University (2025–2026).

Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 (114-1, 114-2) · Required general education · Taught in Mandarin

Natural Science and Artificial Intelligence

自然科學與人工智慧導論 · first-year non-STEM students · 181 students

A required course that brings first-year students from outside the sciences to questions in the natural sciences, and to what AI can add to them. One session, for example, follows how plants communicate through scent, how insects read those chemical signals with their antennae, and how AI is beginning to decode that language. The emphasis is on scientific reasoning and conceptual continuity rather than tool-specific training.

Want to go further?

The questions in these courses, from ribosome collisions to ER stress and plant stress physiology, are the ones the lab works on. Undergraduates can join through independent study (專題研究) or a summer project, and graduate students apply through the Institute of Biotechnology. See Join the lab, or write to yuehcho@ntu.edu.tw.
