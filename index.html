Want to go further?

The questions in these courses, from ribosome collisions to ER stress and plant stress physiology, are the ones the lab works on. Undergraduates can join through independent study (專題研究) or a summer project, and graduate students apply through the Institute of Biotechnology. See Join the lab, or write to yuehcho@ntu.edu.tw.
