Biotechnology Core Techniques

Bachelor Program of Biotechnology and Food and Nutrition, National Taiwan University · mainly undergraduates

A full-day, hands-on lab course organized around the central dogma, moving from DNA to RNA to protein and training students in the core techniques of molecular biotechnology. Ryan leads the DNA module (weeks 1–8), from micropipetting through cloning, transformation, colony PCR, and Southern blotting. The course is co-taught with Prof. Jen-Chih Chen and Prof. Shu-Han Yu.
