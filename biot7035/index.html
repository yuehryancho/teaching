Plant Physiology and Stress Responses

Institute of Biotechnology, National Taiwan University

This course provides a systematic introduction to plant physiological function and the mechanisms by which plants respond to environmental stress. The first half establishes core physiology: water and nutrient relations, photosynthetic and respiratory metabolism, hormonal signaling, and the regulation of growth and development. The second half focuses on how plants perceive and respond to drought, salinity, temperature, oxidative, and biotic stresses, integrating molecular signaling with whole-plant physiology.

The course emphasizes mechanistic understanding and critical reading of the primary literature. Later sessions introduce physiological measurement and omics approaches (transcriptomics, proteomics and metabolomics), connecting classical physiology to modern stress-biology research. Lectures are combined with literature discussion to build your ability to evaluate research and design experiments independently.
